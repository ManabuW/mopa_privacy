Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Ham's Studio ("we", "us" or "our") handles users' personal information and device information in the Android app "Mopa" (the "App").

1. Information We Access and How We Use It

The App accesses the information listed below. None of this information is sent to or stored on any external server.

  • Calendar information (READ_CALENDAR permission)
    The App reads event titles, start times and all-day flags from the calendars on your device. This information is used only to show your events on the overlay and to send reminder notifications. The App never writes to your calendar.
  • Foreground app information (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS permission)
    The App obtains the package name of the app you are currently using in order to control the overlay on a per-app basis (show, dim or hide). Package names are processed only on your device and are never sent externally.
  • Images from your photo library (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE permission)
    The App accesses image files that you choose to register as custom icons. Selected images are stored only on your device (Room database) and are never sent externally.
  • Memos
    Memos you create and edit in the App are stored only on your device (Room database). They are never sent to external servers.
  • App settings
    Settings such as display size, opacity and reminder preferences are stored only on your device (Android DataStore).

2. Payment Information

The App offers subscription purchases (Mopa Premium) through Google Play. All payments are processed by Google Play Billing, and we do not collect or store payment information such as credit card numbers. The App uses the Google Play Billing Library and an internet connection (INTERNET permission) to check your purchase status.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Personally identifiable information such as your name, email address or phone number
  • Location information (GPS)
  • Camera or microphone data
  • Your device contacts
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA / GAID)
  • Crash reports or analytics data

4. Sharing with Third Parties

We do not sell, provide or disclose users' personal information or device information to third parties, except where disclosure is required by law.

5. Permissions Used

  • SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW (Display over other apps)
    Used for the overlay, which is the App's core feature. It is not used until you explicitly grant it.
  • READ_CALENDAR (Read calendar)
    Used to retrieve your events (overlay display and reminders). The App never writes to your calendar.
  • PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS (Usage access)
    Used for the per-app display control feature. It is not used until you explicitly grant it.
  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Access to images)
    Used only when you choose a custom icon. The App accesses only the images you select and never scans your entire library.
  • USE_EXACT_ALARM / SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM (Exact alarms)
    Used to trigger reminders precisely at the specified time.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE / FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE (Foreground service)
    Used to keep the overlay displayed on screen.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Send notifications)
    Used to send reminder notifications.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED (Run at startup)
    Used to start the service when your device boots, if auto-start is enabled.
  • INTERNET (Internet access)
    Used only to check your purchase status via Google Play Billing. It is never used to send user data externally.

6. Data Retention

Memos, custom icons and app settings are stored on your device until you uninstall the App. Uninstalling the App deletes all of this data.

7. Security

Because the information the App accesses is never sent to external servers, there is no risk of data leakage over the network. Data on your device is protected by Android's standard security features (app sandboxing).

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from users under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we do, we will publish the latest version on this page. If there are significant changes, we will notify you within the App.
